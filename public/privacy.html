Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

PhaseTwo is a cycle-tracking and planning app. It handles sensitive health information, so this page explains plainly what we collect, why, who else sees it, and how you can remove it.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in, your email address and, if you use Google sign-in, your name and Google account identifier. You can also use PhaseTwo without signing in.
  • Cycle information. Period start dates, cycle length, period length and settings such as your role, theme and reminder preference.
  • Daily check-ins. Pain score and location, bleeding, symptoms, impact on daily life and related notes you choose to enter.
  • Partner sharing. If you invite a partner, only your cycle dates and length are shared with the person who accepts your invite code. Your check-ins are not shared.
  • Ask Bella messages. The questions you type to Bella, and a count of daily messages used to apply a daily limit.
  • Basic usage data. Anonymous page-view statistics through Vercel Analytics.

How we use it

  • To show your cycle, predictions, calendar, history and reports.
  • To send period reminders 2 days and 1 day before your predicted period, if you turn them on.
  • To answer your questions in Ask Bella.
  • To keep the service secure and working, including bot protection on sign-in.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where your data is stored

  • Signed in: your data is stored in your account with our database and sign-in provider, Supabase. Access is restricted to your own account.
  • Not signed in: your data stays in your browser on your device and is not sent to us.
  • Reminders are produced on your device. No notification service receives your cycle dates.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase for sign-in, database and server functions.
  • Google if you choose Sign in with Google.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated sign-ups.
  • OpenRouter and its model providers to generate Bella's replies. Your message is sent to them. When you ask Bella about your own history, a short summary of recent patterns (such as cycle length and symptom trends) is included. Your free-text notes are not sent. Bella shares information, not medical advice.
  • Vercel for hosting and anonymous analytics.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete period starts and check-ins from History.
  • Delete your symptom history from the check-in section.
  • Delete your account from Settings. This permanently removes your account and synced data.
  • Turn off reminders in Settings, or in your browser or device notification settings.
  • Disconnect a partner in Settings to stop sharing cycle dates.

Retention and security

We keep your data until you delete it or your account. Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by per-account access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong sign-in and keep your device secure.

Children

PhaseTwo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, delete the account from Settings.

Medical disclaimer

PhaseTwo and Bella provide general information and estimates. They are not a diagnosis or a substitute for a healthcare professional. If you are unwell or worried, see a clinician.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
