Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026 · Version 2026-10-24

The short version

  • PhaseTwo helps you track your menstrual cycle, log symptoms, plan around your cycle and, if you choose, share your cycle dates with one partner.
  • If you don’t sign in, everything you enter stays on your own device.
  • If you sign in, your data is saved to your account so it works across your devices.
  • Your symptom check-ins and notes are never shown to a partner. A partner only sees cycle dates and length.
  • We don’t sell your data, show ads, or build advertising profiles.
  • You can delete your account and all of your saved data yourself, at any time, in Settings.

1. Who we are

PhaseTwo (“PhaseTwo”, “we”, “us”) is a web app at https://phasetwo.space. It is operated by Korir Elias, an individual based in Nairobi, Kenya, who is the data controller for the information described in this policy.

Contact for anything privacy-related: eliashubber1@gmail.com.

2. What information we collect

We collect only what you type into PhaseTwo, plus the technical data needed to run it. Here is every category.

2.1 Account details (only if you sign in)

  • Email sign-in: your email address.
  • Google sign-in: your name, email address, Google account identifier and the link to your Google profile picture, as provided by Google. We don’t display or use the picture.
  • A sign-in session (a token that keeps you signed in) and your sign-in times.

Why: to create your account, sign you in, and keep your data separate from everyone else’s. PhaseTwo has no passwords.

2.2 Optional profile details and settings

  • A display name, your age, and a city (all optional), and your time zone.
  • Your settings: whether you track your own cycle or view a partner’s, light/dark theme, and whether period reminders are on.

Why: to personalise the app (for example greeting you by name or showing times correctly). We don’t require any of these.

2.3 Cycle information

  • The period start dates you record, your usual cycle length and your usual period length.

Why: to show where you are in your cycle and estimate your next period. Estimates are calculated from what you entered and are not a medical measurement.

2.4 Symptom check-ins (health information)

  • For each day you choose to log: the date, cycle day and phase, a pain score, where it hurts, symptoms, bleeding level, how much it affected your day and which areas of life, whether pain happened outside your period, whether painkillers helped, and any free-text notes you write.

Why: so you can see patterns over time and prepare for a healthcare appointment. You decide what to log. This is health information, which is sensitive – see section 5.

2.5 Planner tasks

  • The tasks you add (title and status) and the cycle phases you pair them with.

2.6 Partner Advice chat

  • The messages you type to the Partner Advice assistant and its replies. They are saved to your account so the conversation is there next time. To write a reply, the assistant receives your message, recent messages from that chat, and a short context line: the cycle day, phase and cycle length, and the name label of the person whose cycle it is.

2.7 Partner sharing (optional)

  • When you invite a partner we store the email address you entered, a one-time invite code, its expiry date and whether it was accepted. When it is accepted we record which account accepted it.
  • What your partner can see: your period start dates, cycle length, period length, the name label you chose, and your time zone.
  • What your partner can never see: symptom check-ins, notes, tasks, chat messages, your email address, age or city.

You can stop sharing at any time (Settings → Partner sync → Stop sharing), and the partner loses access immediately.

2.8 Ask Bella (AI health-education assistant)

  • The messages you type to Bella, plus up to the last 12 messages of that conversation, are sent to an AI provider so it can write a reply.
  • If – and only if – you ask about your own patterns or an appointment summary, we also include a summary of your recent tracking: cycle and period length, counts of cycles showing certain patterns, and up to 12 recent check-ins from the last six months (date, cycle day, phase, pain score, pain locations, symptoms, bleeding, daily impact).
  • We never send your notes, name, email address or account ID to the AI provider.
  • We do not save your Bella conversations. They exist only in your open browser tab. We keep a count of how many messages you send each day (to enforce a daily limit) and a technical log with the time and success/failure of each request – not its content.

2.9 Your agreement

  • The version of these documents you accepted and when. If you are signed in, this is saved to your account.

2.10 Technical and usage data

  • Our hosting and database providers automatically process your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages or requests made, in server logs used for security, reliability and fixing problems.
  • We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views (pages visited, referring site, country, browser, operating system and device type). It does not use cookies and does not follow you across other sites. It only runs after you have accepted these documents.

2.11 Information stored on your own device

  • Your browser’s local storage holds your sign-in session, a copy of your data so the app loads quickly, your preferences and a record that you accepted these documents.
  • If you don’t sign in (guest mode), all your cycle, symptom and planner data stays only in this browser. We never receive it. Clearing your browser’s site data erases it.

2.12 Period reminders

  • If you turn reminders on, your browser asks for notification permission. While the app is open, reminders are created on your device.
  • If you are signed in and your browser supports it, we also store a push subscription for that browser (the push-service address, the encryption keys it issued and the browser type). Once a day our server reads your predicted period date and time zone and sends a notification 2 days and 1 day before it, through your browser’s push service (for example Google, Mozilla or Apple), which therefore learns that a notification was delivered to that browser. The notification text, such as “Your period is expected tomorrow”, can appear on a locked screen.
  • Turning reminders off or signing out on a device removes that device’s subscription. Deleting your account removes all of them.

2.13 Emails we send

  • Sign-in links, and partner invitations (sent only when a user types an address and asks us to). If someone invites you, we hold your email address only for that invitation until it is accepted, revoked or expires after 14 days.

3. What we do not collect

PhaseTwo does not collect

  • Your precise location or GPS. (“City” is optional text you type; we never read your device location.)
  • Your camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • Your contacts, calendar, messages or other apps’ data.
  • Payment card or bank details. PhaseTwo is free and takes no payments.
  • Government ID numbers or biometrics.
  • Advertising IDs, or data for advertising or cross-site tracking.
  • Your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar or any other Google service data.

PhaseTwo does not do this

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not build marketing profiles.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models.
  • We do not send marketing emails. We only email you sign-in links and invitations you asked for.
  • We do not show your symptoms or notes to a partner.

One caution: notes and chat boxes are free text, so they can contain anything you write. Please don’t type other people’s personal details (for example someone’s full name, address or health information) unless you need to.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide PhaseTwo: show your cycle, predictions, history, planner and reports; sync your data across your devices; send sign-in links and invitations.
  • To power partner sharing, only for the person you invite.
  • To generate replies from Ask Bella and Partner Advice.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse (for example daily message limits and bot protection) and fix bugs.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to your requests.

We do not make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Health information and your consent

Information about your cycle and symptoms is health information. Laws such as Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019 and the EU/UK GDPR treat it as sensitive and require your explicit consent.

  • Before you use PhaseTwo we ask you to tick a separate box giving that consent. Without it you can’t use the app.
  • Our legal bases are your consent (health information and optional features), performing the service you asked for (account and sync), and our legitimate interests in security and preventing abuse.
  • You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting entries or deleting your account in Settings. Withdrawing doesn’t affect anything done before, but we will stop processing what you delete.

6. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers that run PhaseTwo, with a partner you choose, and where the law requires.

  • Supabase – database, sign-in and server functions. Stores all signed-in data. Our database is hosted in the EU (Ireland).
  • Vercel – hosts the website and provides cookieless page-view analytics.
  • Google – sign-in if you choose “Continue with Google”, and Google Fonts, which the app loads for its typefaces (Google therefore sees your IP address and browser details when you open the app).
  • Resend – delivers partner invitation emails. Receives the recipient’s email address, the sender’s name and the invitation content.
  • OpenRouter and the AI model provider it routes to – receive your Ask Bella and Partner Advice messages (see 2.6 and 2.8) to write replies.
  • Cloudflare – the app loads a bot-protection script from Cloudflare, so Cloudflare sees your IP address and browser details.
  • A partner you invite – only the cycle dates and length described in 2.7.
  • Authorities or others – if required by law, or to protect people’s safety or our rights. We will limit what we disclose to what is required.

These providers act on our instructions to run PhaseTwo, except where they are independent controllers of their own services (for example Google).

If PhaseTwo is ever transferred to another operator, we will tell you first, and your data will stay covered by this policy unless you agree otherwise.

7. Google sign-in and Google user data

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us. We use your email and name only to create your account and sign you in. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts or any other Google data.

PhaseTwo’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we don’t use Google user data for advertising, we don’t sell it, we don’t transfer it to others except as needed to provide PhaseTwo or comply with law, and humans do not read it except with your consent, for security, or to comply with law.

You can remove PhaseTwo’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Where your data is stored and international transfers

Signed-in data is stored in the EU (Ireland) with Supabase. Our website is delivered through Vercel’s global network. Some providers (such as the AI provider, Resend, Cloudflare and Google) may process data outside Kenya and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. We use providers that offer contractual or other safeguards for such transfers. By accepting these documents you agree to these transfers.

9. How long we keep information, and deleting it

  • Signed-in data is kept until you delete it or delete your account.
  • You can delete individual symptom entries in the app.
  • Delete account (Settings → Delete my account) permanently removes your account and saved data: sign-in identity, profile and settings, cycle information, symptom check-ins, planner tasks, chat history, partner invitations and links, your agreement record and usage counters. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. Copies in our providers’ routine backups are overwritten on their normal schedule.
  • If your partner link is removed or either account is deleted, sharing ends.
  • Unaccepted invitations expire after 14 days and are deleted if you revoke them or delete your account.
  • Bella conversations are not saved. Provider server logs are kept for the short periods set by each provider.
  • Guest-mode data lives only in your browser: clear your site data to remove it.
  • If you can’t use the in-app tools, email us and we will delete your data.

10. Security

  • Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and our infrastructure providers encrypt stored data.
  • Access rules in the database mean each signed-in account can read only its own records, and partner sharing exposes only the specific fields in 2.7.
  • There are no passwords to steal; sign-in uses a one-time email link or Google.
  • We only access your information when needed to operate, secure or support the service, or when the law requires.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Your rights

Under Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019 and similar laws (such as the EU/UK GDPR), you may:

  • ask for access to the information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong (most of it you can edit in the app);
  • delete your information (Settings → Delete my account, or email us);
  • object to or ask us to restrict certain processing;
  • ask for a copy of your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time.

Email eliashubber1@gmail.com to use these rights. We may need to confirm it is really you, and we will respond promptly and within the time required by law. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya) or your local data-protection authority.

12. Children

PhaseTwo is for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we ask you to confirm your age before using it. If we learn that we hold information from someone under 18, we will delete it.

13. Cookies and local storage

PhaseTwo does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for the purposes in 2.11. Vercel Web Analytics is cookieless. Google (fonts) and Cloudflare (bot protection) may use their own technical storage when their content loads; see their policies above.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update the date and version at the top when we change this policy. If a change is significant, PhaseTwo will ask you to read and accept the new version before you continue. Earlier versions are available on request.
